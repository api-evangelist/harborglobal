# Harbor Global

> Harbor Global is a global professional-services firm delivering technology, operations, strategy, and intelligence consulting to law firms, corporate legal departments, and government legal teams. Harbor is a portfolio company of Norwest Venture Partners. This is an API Evangelist network profile — Harbor Global is a services company and does not publish a public API, developer portal, or SDKs.
